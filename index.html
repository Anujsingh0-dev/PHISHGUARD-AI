    Real-World Generalization & Robust Feature Extraction

    Our exploratory analysis revealed that naive models often overfit to dataset artifacts (such as mandatory www. prefixes in benchmark data).

    Our Engineering Solution: We implemented canonical normalization that standardizes URLs and strips www. prefixes before evaluating subdomain hierarchy, coupled with high-reputation domain verification and brand impersonation heuristic guards.
